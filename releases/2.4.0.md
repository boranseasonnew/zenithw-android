Progressive metadata previews, a reusable Python metadata runtime, safer cancellation and yt-dlp 2026.10.21 stable.

- Open download configuration immediately when pasting or sharing a complete link. Title, artwork and duration placeholders are replaced only by real source data.
- Fetch a small public YouTube preview in parallel with one complete yt-dlp extraction. Sessions, proxies and explicit IP-family preferences keep their normal extraction path.
- Reuse the interpreter and safe anonymous extractor objects, cache completed previews briefly and reuse verified metadata snapshots for downloading.
- Avoid unnecessary format selection and FFmpeg initialization while inspecting resolved videos. Preserve yt-dlp's normal handling of redirects and playlists.
- Protect replacement links from stale results, make cancellation release a blocked metadata worker and retain download choices as information arrives.
- Add separate timing events for panel layout, real title, image decode, duration, format readiness, process startup, imports, extraction and network response headers. Logs omit URLs and credentials.

Measured results and validation details: [performance report](https://github.com/boranseasonnew/zenithw-android/blob/main/docs/metadata-performance-2.4.0.md). The supplied YouTube panel was visible in 255 ms and its real title arrived in 617 ms in the CI benchmark. Full YouTube formats were blocked by CI authentication checks; no physical-device verification was performed. Performance targets are not guarantees; source authentication, network conditions and device speed still apply.

Seal v1.13.1 was studied as a reference. No Seal source or visual assets are bundled; see the comparison and third-party notices in this repository.
